Metered baseline kWh for the same days

How it works. Every minute, the room's air and building mass gain heat from the walls, air leakage, people (75 W each plus moisture), lights and the sun. The AC removes heat and moisture using its own thermostat and 3-minute compressor protection; efficiency drops on hot days. The device can only do what a remote can: on/off, setpoint and mode.

  • Baseline: staff switch on at 08:00 on weekdays and off at 17:30. Outside those hours the AC goes on when people arrive and is left running after they leave.
  • Device: starts at 24 °C, steps up toward the band's top when humidity is under 60%, uses Dry mode above 65%, steps down when the room runs warm, sets back after the room empties, then switches off. When a warm room fills it boost-cools at the band's bottom. People who feel warm may press the remote; the device honours that for 45 minutes.
  • Calibrate by changing AC size, number of ACs and seats until the simulated baseline is within 15% of your metered kWh.
